# First Cats update of 2025

## What is Cats even?
Cats is my passion project that some how came to me on the drive to school. I started developing it in 2024, You should check it out [here](https://github.com/AdamAlNajar/cats!) before continuing.

## How is Cats?
Cats had many changes, many changes of ideas, of code structure. Originally, it was supposed to be multiplayer and exploration based, but now it has become an competition-based leaderboard system game. 

## When will cats release?
It will be released soon, maybe during 2025 summer. It all depends on how my school decides to work

## How was development?
Lets say... i struggled.. a lot, Dialogs, Collisions, Maps, The mechanics. I would've developed it in godot if i wanted to, but as i didnt want to give up, i continued the project in haxeflixel (which is a very cool framework after you get used to its quirks)

## What are you working on now?
